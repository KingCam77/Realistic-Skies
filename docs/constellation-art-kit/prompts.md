# Constellation art kit

For each constellation upload its PNG to the image model together with the prompt. Pictures: north up, east left, exactly as in the Sea Legends scroll.

**Common part of every prompt**

> Style: an old sailor's star atlas, hand-drawn engraving / ink line art in dark sepia, fine hatching, no colour fills, white background, no text, no frame, no extra stars. IMPORTANT: draw the figure AROUND the black star dots. Keep every black dot exactly where it is, the same size, and do not add, remove or move any dot. The grey lines only show how the stars are joined and may be covered by the drawing. Keep the image size and composition unchanged.
